Company Profile & Purpose

Mena’s Nest at a Glance

“To deliver comfort, care, and convenience across the key spaces where people live, eat, and gather.”

Our Vision: To be a trusted household and hospitality brand known for excellence, authenticity, and warm Ghanaian hospitality.

Our Mission: To provide top-tier catering, healthy foods, and comprehensive home-care services that simplify lives and enrich everyday experiences.

At Mena’s Nest, we bring together two dedicated specialist arms: LACUIZINEE, our food and beverage arm providing corporate & private catering, wholesome prepared meals, dietary planning, and artisanal beverages/teas; and AKWAABA FIE, our home readiness arm providing pre-arrival deep cleaning, fridge stocking, guest-room preparation, and our signature "Welcome Home" suite for Ghanaians in the diaspora.

✈️

🧼

48-Hour Deep Sanitization

Step 03
