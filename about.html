Chapter 1: The Hearth & The Soil

A Kitchen Steeped in Ancestral Wisdom & Wholesome Science

Long before Mena’s Nest grew into Accra’s premier dual-pillar hospitality brand, Aunt Mosa began with a simple, stubborn belief: food must heal, comfort, and energize.

Watching modern commercial foods become laden with chemical stabilizers, excessive refined sugars, and artificial flavorings, she turned back to the rich botanical bounty of West African soil. She spent years perfecting traditional cold infusions of indigenous Hibiscus Sabdariffa (sobolo), nutrient-dense baobab pulp, tart tamarind, and warm grains of selim (hwentia).

What began as private dietary tonics for busy professionals, pregnant mothers, and recovering elders quickly became an in-demand culinary movement. That passion sparked the creation of LACUIZINEE—delivering artisanal beverages, daily meal subscription boxes, and bespoke catering across Accra.

“When you drink our sobolo or eat our meals, your body immediately recognizes the purity of the ingredients.”

— Aunt Mosa, Founder & Executive Chef

Chapter 2: Midnight at Kotoka

The Heartbreaking Story That Built Akwaaba Fie

In December 2021, close friends of Aunt Mosa flew into Accra from London with two young toddlers for the holidays. After 14 hours of travel and airport queues, they opened the front door of their home at 1:30 AM to find:

  • Stale, trapped tropical humidity and dusty furniture.
  • Air conditioners blowing hot air because the prepaid meter had depleted.
  • An empty refrigerator with zero drinking water or warm food for the crying children.

Aunt Mosa packed hot jollof rice, fresh hibiscus drinks, chilled dispenser water, clean bedsheets, and rushed across Accra in the dead of night to rescue their first night.

Driving home at dawn, she made a vow: No diaspora family or traveler should ever experience that again. That was the birth of AKWAABA FIE—the ultimate pre-arrival home sanitization, utility verification, pantry provisioning, and airport concierge service in Ghana.
