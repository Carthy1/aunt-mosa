The Akwaaba "Welcome Home" Suite

Designed specifically for Ghanaians living in the UK, USA, Europe, and Canada visiting home. Avoid the stress of landing at Kotoka Airport only to face dusty rooms, empty refrigerators, unmade beds, and dead AC remote batteries. We handle everything 48 hours prior.

🧼

1. Deep Scrubbing & Sanitization

Thorough cleaning of tiles, deep scrubbing of bathrooms and water heaters, descaling faucets, wiping cobwebs, sanitizing AC air filters, and opening windows for fresh airflow before sealing with welcoming scents.

  • Kitchen degreasing & appliance sanitization
  • Dusting all blinds, shutters & ceiling fans
  • Floor polishing and patio pressure wash
🧺

2. Hotel-Grade Bedding & Linens

We wash, sun-dry, iron, and expertly dress your master bedroom and guest suites with crisp cotton linens, plush pillows, fresh towels, bath mats, and luxury botanical toiletries ready in each bathroom.

  • High thread-count duvet & pillow dressing
  • Freshly laundered bath and hand towels
  • Natural lemongrass & lavender room scenting
🥭

3. Fresh Market & Pantry Stocking

Send us your shopping list or let our concierges source fresh Ghanaian staples: fresh eggs, tea bread, organic butter, cold milk, seasonal mangoes/pineapples, bottled spring water, and snacks waiting in the pantry.

  • Bottled mineral water chilled in fridge
  • Fresh local tropical fruits & plantain chips
  • Custom supermarket & market list fulfillment
🍲

4. Hot Arrival Feast & Artisanal Drinks

Powered by our sister kitchen LACUIZINEE: A warm pot of authentic Ghanaian Jollof rice with grilled chicken, light goat soup, or spicy kelewele, plus chilled bottles of handcrafted hibiscus Sobolo.

  • Small-batch clove-infused chilled Sobolo
  • Fresh gourmet meal ready upon arrival
  • No midnight supermarket or takeaway scramble
